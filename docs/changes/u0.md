# Akalabeth on the cart: what is different

Akalabeth: World of Doom (c) 1980 Richard Garriott, the game before Ultima I. The cart
runs the C64 conversion by Andrea Schincaglia (code and conversion), Antonello Molella
(idea and testing) and David Vigh (graphics), 2016, a faithful port of the Apple II
original. Everything you see and play is that program, apart from what is listed here.

## Which disk the build needs

The build reads Genesis Project's release of the conversion ("Akalabeth - World of Doom
+4", 2016; its disk is named "accept no limits"): the main file `AKALABETH+4  /GP` and the
graphics library `LIB`. It is the only release the build knows.

## No disk at all

- The program and its graphics library are on the cart. The game never loads anything,
  so it needs no drive and never touches one.
- **The instructions have no pictures.** Answer Y to "INSTRUCTIONS (Y/N)", and the
  original shows three pictures, one after the other, before the instructions' text; they
  are 30 KB on the disk, and the cart has no room for them. The text is all there, and
  comes right after the title.
- The cart does not run Genesis Project's intro.

## The trainer's options

Genesis Project's release asks four questions as the game starts ("Fixed and trained by
Wertstahl"). On the cart these are options on Akalabeth's page in the launcher instead,
and the game starts without asking:

| Key | Option | Off |
|---|---|---|
| F | Food used while walking | walking (outdoors and in the dungeons) costs no food |
| G | Gold spent when buying | the shops take no gold |
| A | Attacks can miss | the player always hits and always kills |
| H | Monsters can hurt you | monsters do no damage |

All four are on (the game as it was) on a new cart, and the cart remembers them.

## Saving

Akalabeth saves nothing, on the cart as on the disk: a game lasts until it is switched
off. Its page has no saved game, no Journey onward, no export, import or reset.
